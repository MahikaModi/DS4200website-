Legends & Gear

The players who shaped the sport and the equipment that makes it all possible.

The Greatest Players of All Time

Badminton has produced some of the most extraordinary athletes in the history of sport, and understanding their legacies gives you a deeper appreciation for how the game is played today. The sport has been dominated at various points by players from China, Indonesia, Denmark, Japan, Malaysia, and India, each bringing their own style and philosophy to the court. What all of the greatest players share is an almost supernatural combination of court vision, physical conditioning, and mental toughness that allows them to perform under the most intense pressure imaginable.

The modern era of badminton has been defined by fierce rivalries and incredible individual achievements. Players have pushed the boundaries of what is physically possible on a badminton court, developing new techniques and training methods that have transformed the sport. The rise of badminton in South and Southeast Asia has brought millions of new fans to the sport, while traditional powerhouses like China and Denmark continue to produce world-class talent. Whether you follow men's singles, women's singles, or doubles, there is always a compelling storyline and an emerging star to watch on the BWF World Tour.

🏆 Lin Dan — "Super Dan"

Widely considered the greatest badminton player of all time, Lin Dan of China won two Olympic gold medals, five World Championships, and dominated the sport for over a decade. His aggressive playing style and flair for dramatic moments made him a global icon.

🏆 Viktor Axelsen — The Modern King

Denmark's Viktor Axelsen has been the dominant force in men's singles since 2020, winning Olympic gold in Tokyo and multiple World Tour titles. His towering frame, devastating smash, and calm demeanor make him nearly unbeatable on his best days.

🏆 P.V. Sindhu — India's Pride

P.V. Sindhu became the first Indian woman to win an Olympic silver medal in badminton and followed it up with a bronze in Tokyo. She has inspired millions of young players across India to take up the sport.

🏆 Tai Tzu-Ying — The Magician

Known for her extraordinary deception and wrist control, Tai Tzu-Ying of Chinese Taipei is one of the most entertaining players to watch in any sport. Her ability to disguise shots and change direction at the last millisecond is unmatched.

Essential Badminton Gear

  1. Racket — The most important piece of equipment. Top brands include Yonex, Li-Ning, and Victor. Rackets vary in weight, balance, and flexibility depending on playing style.
  2. Shuttlecocks — Feather shuttles (made from goose feathers) are used in competitive play for their flight stability. Nylon shuttles are more durable for casual games.
  3. Court Shoes — Badminton-specific shoes with gum rubber soles provide essential grip on indoor courts and protect against ankle injuries during quick lateral movements.
  4. Grip Tape — Overgrips absorb sweat and improve racket control. Most players replace their grip tape after every few sessions.
  5. Strings — String tension and type significantly affect power and control. Higher tension gives more control but less power, while lower tension adds repulsion for harder smashes.

Why You Should Try Badminton

  • One of the best full-body cardiovascular workouts you can get from a sport
  • Improves hand-eye coordination, reflexes, and agility
  • Low barrier to entry — you only need a racket, shuttlecock, and a net to start
  • Can be played indoors year-round, perfect for Boston winters
  • Extremely social — doubles is the most popular format and requires teamwork and communication
  • Active competitive community at Northeastern and across the Boston area
